Admin Help Center

Guides and answers for managing staff, patients, appointments, and resources efficiently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Go to the "Staff Management" section in your dashboard. Click "Add Staff" to register a new team member, or use the edit/delete icons to update or remove existing records.

Open the "Patient Management" section, search for the patient, and click the "Edit" icon to update details. All changes are logged for compliance purposes.

In the "Appointment Management" module, you can view, reschedule, or cancel appointments. You can also set reminders and allocate them to available staff members.

Go to "Inventory Management" to view stock levels, track usage, and create reorder requests for supplies such as test kits and medications.

Navigate to the "Reports" section and select the desired date range and metrics. Reports can be exported as PDF or Excel for sharing and record keeping.
