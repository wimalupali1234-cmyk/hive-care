About HIV Support Portal

Our mission is to provide comprehensive support and resources for individuals affected by HIV.

Our Mission

The HIV Support Portal was founded in 2020 with the goal of creating a safe, stigma-free space for individuals living with or affected by HIV. We believe everyone deserves access to accurate information, quality care, and emotional support.

Our Vision

We envision a world where HIV is no longer a barrier to living a full, healthy life. Through education, advocacy, and support services, we work toward eliminating stigma and improving quality of life for all affected by HIV.
