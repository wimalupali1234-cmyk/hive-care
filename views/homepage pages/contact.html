Emergency?

If you're experiencing a medical emergency, please call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room immediately.

For urgent HIV-related concerns, call our 24/7 hotline: 1-800-HIV-HELP

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. Urgent requests are prioritized and typically receive a response within 2-4 hours.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes, absolutely. All information shared with us is kept strictly confidential and protected under HIPAA guidelines. We never share your personal information without your explicit consent.

Can I request anonymous support?

Yes, you can request anonymous support. While we encourage providing contact information for follow-up, we understand the need for privacy and will work with you to ensure your comfort.

What languages do you support?

We currently offer support in English, Spanish, French, and Sinhala. If you need support in another language, please let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate you.
