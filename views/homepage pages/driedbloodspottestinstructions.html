Dried Blood Spot Test Instructions

Follow these step-by-step instructions to collect your dried blood spot sample for HIV testing. Results will be available within 3-5 business days through our secure portal.

1

Receive and Check Your Collection Kit

When you receive your dried blood spot collection kit, carefully check that all components are included:

  • Filter paper card with printed circles
  • Sterile lancets (usually 2-3)
  • Alcohol wipes
  • Gauze pads
  • Adhesive bandages
  • Prepaid return envelope
  • Biohazard bag
  • Instructions and consent forms

Ensure the kit is not expired and all packaging is intact.

2

Complete Required Forms

Before collecting your sample:

  • Fill out the personal information form completely
  • Sign the consent form for testing
  • Provide contact information for results delivery
  • Note any medications you're currently taking
  • Record the date and time of collection

Use a black or blue pen and write clearly.

3

Prepare for Sample Collection

Set up your collection area:

  • Choose a clean, well-lit area
  • Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and warm water
  • Dry your hands completely with a clean towel
  • Massage your hands to improve blood circulation
  • Arrange all materials within easy reach
  • Have the filter paper card ready with circles facing up
4

Prepare Your Finger

Select and clean the finger for blood collection:

  • Choose the side of your ring finger or middle finger
  • Clean the chosen finger thoroughly with an alcohol wipe
  • Allow the finger to air dry completely (30-60 seconds)
  • Do not touch the cleaned area after wiping
  • If your hands are cold, warm them under warm water first
5

Collect Blood Drops

Use the lancet to collect blood samples:

  • Remove the lancet cap and press firmly against your fingertip
  • Activate the lancet by pressing the trigger
  • Wipe away the first drop of blood with gauze
  • Allow a large drop of blood to form
  • Gently touch the blood drop to the center of the first circle
  • Allow the blood to soak through to the back of the paper
  • Repeat for remaining circles (usually 4-5 circles total)
6

Ensure Proper Sample Quality

Check that your samples meet quality requirements:

  • Each circle should be completely filled with blood
  • Blood should soak through to the back of the paper
  • Avoid air bubbles or layered blood
  • Do not squeeze blood from your finger directly onto the paper
  • If a circle is not properly filled, collect another drop
  • Apply pressure to your finger with gauze to stop bleeding
7

Dry the Blood Spots

Allow the blood spots to dry properly:

  • Place the filter paper card on a clean, flat surface
  • Keep the card at room temperature
  • Allow to air dry for at least 3 hours (overnight is preferred)
  • Do not use heat, direct sunlight, or a hair dryer
  • Keep away from humidity and moisture
  • Ensure nothing touches the blood spots while drying
8

Package for Shipping

Once the blood spots are completely dry:

  • Place the dried filter paper card in the biohazard bag
  • Include the completed forms in the same bag
  • Seal the biohazard bag securely
  • Place the sealed bag in the prepaid return envelope
  • Ensure all information is included and legible
  • Do not fold or bend the filter paper card

Important Shipping Information

Mail your sample within 24 hours of collection for best results. Samples must reach the laboratory within 14 days of collection. Use only the provided prepaid envelope for shipping.

9

Mail Your Sample

Send your sample to the laboratory:

  • Mail the package on the same day or next business day
  • Use the prepaid envelope provided
  • Drop off at any postal service location
  • Keep a record of when you mailed the sample
  • Note the tracking number if available
10

Dispose of Materials and Follow Up

Clean up and prepare for results:

  • Dispose of used lancets in a puncture-resistant container
  • Put other used materials in regular household waste
  • Wash your hands thoroughly
  • Results will be available in 3-5 business days
  • Check your secure online portal or wait for phone call
  • Contact our support line if you have questions: 1-800-HIV-HELP
11

Receive and Act on Results

When your results are ready:

  • Access results through your secure online account
  • Or receive results via confidential phone call
  • If negative, discuss future testing schedule with healthcare provider
  • If positive, seek confirmatory testing immediately
  • Contact our counseling services for support and guidance
  • Follow up with healthcare providers as recommended
