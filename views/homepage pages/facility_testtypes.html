What is Facility Based Testing?

Facility based testing refers to HIV and related health tests conducted at certified healthcare centers, clinics, or hospitals. These tests are performed by trained professionals using advanced equipment, ensuring accuracy, confidentiality, and immediate support.

Facility based testing often includes pre- and post-test counseling, and is recommended for those seeking comprehensive care and guidance. Our certified facilities provide a safe, private environment where you can receive testing with complete confidentiality.

Viral Load Testing

What it is:What it is: Viral load testing measures the amount of HIV in your blood. It's a crucial test for people living with HIV to monitor their health and treatment effectiveness.

How it's performed: How it's performed: The test is performed by drawing a blood sample from your arm using a needle. The sample is then analyzed in a laboratory using advanced technology to count the number of HIV copies per milliliter of blood. Results are typically available within a few days.

Regular viral load testing is essential for people living with HIV to ensure treatment is working effectively.

STI Testing

What it is: STI testing at a facility includes comprehensive screening for sexually transmitted infections such as syphilis, gonorrhea, chlamydia, and other common STIs.

How it's performed: Depending on the specific STI being tested, samples may be collected via blood draw, urine sample, or swabs from affected areas. Our healthcare professionals ensure the process is comfortable and confidential. Most results are available within 1-3 days.

Early detection and treatment of STIs are crucial for your health and for preventing transmission to others.

Hepatitis C Testing

What it is: Hepatitis C testing detects the presence of the hepatitis C virus, which can cause serious liver damage if left untreated. Testing is especially important for those at higher risk.

How it's performed: The test is performed using a blood sample drawn from your arm. The laboratory checks for hepatitis C antibodies and may also test for the virus itself (HCV RNA). Initial antibody tests provide results quickly, while confirmatory tests may take a few days.

Facility-based Hep C testing ensures accurate results and immediate access to counseling and treatment options if needed.

Hepatitis B Testing

What it is: Hepatitis B testing checks for infection with the hepatitis B virus and can also determine if you have immunity from vaccination or previous infection.

How it's performed: The test requires a blood sample taken from your arm. The laboratory analyzes the blood for hepatitis B surface antigen, antibodies, and other markers. This comprehensive panel helps determine your hepatitis B status and need for vaccination or treatment.

Facility-based Hep B testing provides reliable results and access to vaccination or treatment if needed. It's a key component of comprehensive sexual health care.
