Finger Prick HIV Test Instructions

Follow these step-by-step instructions to perform your finger prick HIV test safely and accurately. The entire process takes about 25-30 minutes.

1

Receive and Check Your Test Kit

When you receive your finger prick HIV test kit, carefully check that all components are included:

  • Test cassette in sealed foil pouch
  • Sterile lancet for finger pricking
  • Alcohol wipe
  • Buffer solution (dropper bottle)
  • Gauze pad or bandage
  • Instructions booklet

Ensure the kit is not expired and all packaging is intact.

2

Prepare for Testing

Before starting the test:

  • Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and warm water
  • Dry your hands completely with a clean towel
  • Massage your hands to improve blood circulation
  • Choose a clean, well-lit area to work
  • Have all components within easy reach
  • Read all instructions carefully before beginning
3

Prepare Your Finger

Select and prepare the finger for blood collection:

  • Choose the side of your ring finger or middle finger
  • Clean the chosen finger with the alcohol wipe
  • Allow the finger to air dry completely (about 30 seconds)
  • Do not touch the cleaned area with anything
  • Warm your hands if they feel cold to improve blood flow
4

Collect Blood Sample

Use the lancet to obtain a blood sample:

  • Remove the lancet cap by twisting it off
  • Press the lancet firmly against the side of your fingertip
  • Press the trigger to activate the lancet
  • Gently squeeze your finger to encourage blood flow
  • Wipe away the first drop of blood with gauze
  • Allow a large drop of blood to form
5

Apply Blood to Test

Remove the test cassette from its pouch and apply blood:

  • Place the test cassette on a flat surface
  • Touch the blood drop to the sample well (marked with "S")
  • Allow the blood to fill the well completely
  • Apply pressure to your finger with gauze to stop bleeding
  • Apply a bandage if needed
6

Add Buffer Solution

Add the buffer solution to process the test:

  • Wait 1 minute after applying blood
  • Add exactly 2 drops of buffer solution to the same sample well
  • Do not add more or less than 2 drops
  • Start your timer immediately after adding buffer
  • Place the test on a flat surface and do not move it
7

Wait for Results

Wait for the test to process:

  • Set a timer for exactly 15 minutes
  • Do not read results before 15 minutes
  • Do not read results after 20 minutes
  • You will see liquid moving across the test window
  • Colored lines will start to appear in the result window
8

Read Your Results

After exactly 15 minutes, read your results:

  • Negative: Only one line appears at "C" (control line)
  • Positive: Two lines appear at "C" and "T" (control and test lines)
  • Invalid: No line appears at "C", regardless of line at "T"

Any line, no matter how faint, should be considered a line.

Important Note

If your result is positive or invalid, please contact a healthcare provider immediately. A positive self-test result needs to be confirmed with a laboratory test. For immediate support and guidance, call our helpline at 1-800-HIV-HELP.

9

Dispose of Materials Safely

After completing the test:

  • Place the used lancet in a puncture-resistant container
  • Put all other used materials in the provided disposal bag
  • Seal the bag securely
  • Dispose of in regular household waste
  • Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and water
10

Record and Follow Up

Document your test and plan next steps:

  • Record the date, time, and result of your test
  • If negative, discuss future testing schedule with healthcare provider
  • If positive, seek confirmatory testing within 24-48 hours
  • Contact our support services for counseling and guidance
  • Share results with healthcare providers as appropriate
  • Consider informing recent partners if result is positive
