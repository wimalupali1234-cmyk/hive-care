Oral Fluid HIV Test Instructions

Follow these step-by-step instructions to perform your oral fluid HIV test safely and accurately. The entire process takes about 30-40 minutes.

1

Receive and Check Your Test Kit

When you receive your oral fluid HIV test kit, carefully check that all components are included:

  • Test device in sealed pouch
  • Oral fluid collection swab
  • Test tube with developer solution
  • Instructions booklet
  • Timer (or use your phone)

Ensure the kit is not expired and all packaging is intact.

2

Prepare for Testing

Before starting the test:

  • Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and water
  • Do not eat, drink, smoke, or brush your teeth for 30 minutes before testing
  • Find a clean, flat surface to work on
  • Ensure good lighting
  • Read all instructions carefully
3

Collect Oral Fluid Sample

Remove the oral fluid collection swab from its packaging:

  • Hold the swab by the handle, avoid touching the pad
  • Gently rub the swab pad against your upper gum line
  • Then rub against your lower gum line
  • Repeat this process for 2-3 times to ensure adequate sample collection
  • The entire collection should take about 2 minutes
4

Process the Sample

After collecting the oral fluid:

  • Insert the swab into the test tube containing the developer solution
  • Ensure the swab pad is fully immersed in the solution
  • Wait for exactly 1 minute while the swab is in the solution
  • Remove and discard the swab safely
5

Perform the Test

Remove the test device from its sealed pouch:

  • Place the test device on a flat surface
  • Using the dropper cap, add exactly 3 drops of the processed solution into the sample well
  • Start your timer immediately after adding the drops
  • Do not move or disturb the test device
6

Wait for Results

Wait exactly 20 minutes for results:

  • Set a timer for 20 minutes
  • Do not read results before 20 minutes
  • Do not read results after 40 minutes
  • The test window will show colored lines during this time
7

Read Your Results

After exactly 20 minutes, read your results:

  • Negative: Only one line appears (control line)
  • Positive: Two lines appear (control and test lines)
  • Invalid: No lines appear or test line appears without control line

Any line, no matter how faint, should be considered a line.

Important Note

If your result is positive or invalid, please contact a healthcare provider immediately. A positive self-test result needs to be confirmed with a laboratory test. If you need support interpreting results, call our helpline at 1-800-HIV-HELP.

8

Dispose of Materials Safely

After completing the test:

  • Place all used materials in the provided disposal bag
  • Seal the bag securely
  • Dispose of in regular household waste
  • Wash your hands thoroughly
9

Record and Follow Up

Document your test and next steps:

  • Record the date and result of your test
  • If negative, consider regular testing based on your risk factors
  • If positive, seek confirmatory testing immediately
  • Contact our support services for counseling and guidance
  • Follow up with healthcare providers as recommended
