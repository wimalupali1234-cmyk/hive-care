Instructions for PEP users

Follow these instructions when using PEP.

1

General guidance

  • Start Immediately: Take the first dose as soon as possible (ideally within 2 hours, maximum 72 hours).
  • Daily Routine: Take the medicine every day for 28 days at the same time.
  • No Missed Doses: If you forget a dose, take it as soon as you remember. If it’s nearly time for the next one, skip the missed dose (do not double).
  • Complete the Course: Do not stop early, even if you feel fine.
  • Avoid Alcohol & Drugs: These can increase side effects or reduce effectiveness.
  • Follow-Up Tests: HIV tests are needed at baseline, 6 weeks, 3 months, and 6 months.
  • Stay Regular with Check-Ups: HIV testing and kidney function checks are required every 3 months to continue safely.
  • Safe Practices: Use condoms and avoid sharing needles until follow-up confirms your HIV status.

Ensure the medicines are not expired.

2

Possible Side Effects

  • Nausea or upset stomach
  • Fatigue or tiredness
  • Headache
  • Mild dizziness
  • Diarrhea or stomach cramps

Tips to Reduce Side Effects

  • Take with food: Helps reduce nausea or stomach upset.
  • Stay hydrated: Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
  • Rest well: Proper sleep helps with fatigue and headache.
  • Eat light meals: Avoid greasy or heavy foods if you feel nauseous.
  • Speak to your doctor: If side effects are severe or persistent, your doctor may adjust the regimen.
