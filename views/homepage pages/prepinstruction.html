Instructions for PrEP users

Follow these instructions when using PrEP.

1

Guidance for PrEP Users

  • Take your PrEP medication at the same time every day.
  • Use reminders like alarms, apps, or a pillbox to avoid missing doses.
  • Do not skip doses, as this lowers PrEP’s effectiveness.
  • Continue using condoms and safe sex practices for full protection.
  • Visit your healthcare provider every 3 months for HIV testing and monitoring.
  • Inform your doctor about any other medicines or supplements you take.
  • If you miss a dose, take it as soon as you remember unless it is close to your next dose.
  • Do not stop PrEP without consulting your healthcare provider.

Ensure the medicines are not expired.

2

Possible Side Effects of PrEP

  • Mild nausea or stomach upset.
  • Headaches during early use.
  • Small changes in kidney or liver function (monitored by blood tests).
  • Rare, mild decrease in bone strength.

Ways to Reduce Side Effects

  • Take PrEP with food or at night to reduce stomach discomfort.
  • Drink enough water daily to support kidney health.
  • Never stop the medication without medical advice.
  • Report persistent or unusual side effects to your doctor promptly.
