Why We Ask Questions

Throughout your use of HIVeCare, you will be asked some questions to help us understand your risk for HIV and other sexually transmitted diseases (STDs). This will allow us to provide you with personalized support and accurate information.

Phone Number Usage

Your phone number helps us stay in touch with you for any support or information related to HIV services you may need. It is also important for securely accessing your test results.

After Reservation

Once you finish your reservation, you will receive a unique code on the phone number you provided. Please keep this code safe—it will be needed to view your test results and to make future bookings.

Lost Code Recovery

No worries! You can easily generate a new code if you misplace the original one.

Location Permissions

Your phone or computer may ask for permission to access your geographic location. This helps us recommend clinics nearest to you for your convenience.

Privacy and Data Use

The information you provide, including your location and risk-related answers, is used only to improve your experience with the app — like guiding clinic recommendations and understanding how many people use the service. Your privacy is very important to us. All data is kept confidential and anonymous, and it will never be used to identify you personally.

HIV Support Portal

Providing confidential support and resources for people affected by HIV. Your privacy and well-being are our top priorities.
