What is Self Testing?

Self testing refers to HIV tests that you can perform in the privacy of your own home. These tests are designed to be simple, accurate, and provide results within minutes. Self testing empowers individuals to take control of their health while maintaining complete privacy.

Self testing kits are available through our portal and come with detailed instructions, support resources, and access to counseling services if needed. All tests are FDA-approved and provide reliable results when used correctly.

Oral Fluid Based Testing

What it is: Oral fluid-based HIV testing is a non-invasive and convenient method used to detect antibodies to HIV in a person’s saliva. It provides a reliable alternative to blood-based testing, offering a simpler and more comfortable experience for individuals being tested. This approach is widely used in both clinical and community settings due to its ease of collection, minimal risk of biohazard exposure, and the ability to deliver quick results. It plays a significant role in expanding access to HIV screening, especially in outreach and self-testing scenarios.

Finger Prick Blood Based Self Testing

What it is: Finger prick blood-based HIV self-testing is a widely accepted approach that enables individuals to privately check their HIV status using a small blood sample. It is known for its high accuracy and is often included in approved self-testing kits for personal use. This form of testing empowers people to take proactive steps toward their health, especially those who may be hesitant to visit traditional healthcare settings. It plays a key role in early detection and timely linkage to care, contributing to broader public health efforts in HIV prevention and control.

Dried Blood Spot Testing

What it is: Dried blood spot (DBS) testing for HIV is a reliable and widely used method that involves analyzing small samples of blood collected on specialized filter paper. It is particularly useful in remote or resource-limited settings due to its ease of transport, long-term stability, and minimal storage requirements. DBS testing supports large-scale screening and surveillance programs and is often used for confirmatory testing, early infant diagnosis, and monitoring of HIV treatment. Its durability and adaptability make it a valuable tool in expanding HIV testing access across diverse populations.
