Facility Based Testing

Get tested for HIV at a certified healthcare facility with professional support, confidentiality, and immediate counseling if needed. Our facilities are equipped with the latest testing technology and staffed by experienced healthcare professionals.

Self Testing

Order a self-test kit for private, convenient HIV testing at home. Easy instructions and support are available if you need help interpreting results. Perfect for those who prefer privacy and convenience.

HIV Support Portal

Providing confidential support and resources for people affected by HIV. Your privacy and well-being are our top priorities.
